Vocabursters privacy policy

Applies to the Vocabursters app on the App Store and Google Play, and the web app. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

Vocabursters collects no personal data. Nothing a child or grown-up types or does in the app is sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, adverts, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking or chat.

What is stored, and where

Player nicknames, the chosen picture, school year, scores, stars, coins and settings are saved only on the device, inside the app's own storage. We cannot see them. Deleting a player in Grown-ups → Players, or deleting the app, removes them.

The optional backup code in Grown-ups is created on the device. It goes wherever the grown-up chooses to paste it; the app never sends it anywhere.

What is not collected

No names, email addresses, contact details, location, photos, microphone or camera access, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Internet use

The App Store and Google Play apps include every word recording, so they work fully offline and make no network requests while playing. The web version downloads the app and voice recordings from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Vocabursters is made for children aged 5 to 11 and is designed to meet the Apple Kids Category rules, the Google Play Families Policy, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, COPPA and UK GDPR by not collecting personal data at all. The Grown-ups area is behind a simple adult check.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be published here. Questions: see the support page.
