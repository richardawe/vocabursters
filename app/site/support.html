Vocabursters support

Free weekly spelling games for Year 1 to Year 6.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/richardawe/vocabursters/issues. Please say which device you use and which year and week you were on. Don't include a child's name.

Common questions

How do I add a player? On the first screen, tap Add player, type a nickname, pick a picture and the school year.

How do I set the week to match school? Open Grown-ups, answer the grown-ups question, then go to Players.

How do I move progress to a new device? Grown-ups → About & backup → Copy backup code, then paste it into Restore on the new device.

No sound? Check the device isn't muted and the volume is up. The word recordings are inside the app and work offline.

Privacy

Vocabursters collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
